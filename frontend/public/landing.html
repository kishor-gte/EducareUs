FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about Educarrus counselling and admission services.

Which courses does Educarrus help students with?
Educarrus provides complete admission guidance across Medical (MBBS/BDS), Engineering (B.Tech/BE), Pharmacy (B.Pharm), Ayurveda (BAMS), Management (MBA/BBA), and other recognized professional degree programs.
Can you help me choose the right course based on my profile?
Yes, absolutely! Our 1-on-1 career counselling evaluates your Class 12 marks, entrance exam scores (NEET/JEE/CET), personal interests, and long-term career goals to suggest optimal degree pathways.
Do you assist with college selection and shortlisting?
Yes. We help students shortlist recognized and accredited colleges based on academic cutoff, budget expectations, campus infrastructure, hostel safety, and placement track record.
Do you help with application and counselling documentation?
Yes. Our dedicated advisors guide you through filling online application forms, compiling required verification documents, and tracking state & central counselling rounds.
Can parents participate in the counselling session?
We strongly encourage parents to join all counselling sessions! College selection is a major family decision, and we address all parental concerns regarding fees, safety, and recognition.
Is counselling available both online and offline?
Yes! We offer face-to-face counselling at our main office as well as seamless online video calls and phone consultations for students across India.
